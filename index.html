Opening transmission

Some machines stop speaking. Their discoveries do not.

Across the solar system, human-made explorers remain where their missions ended: rovers resting on Mars, landers on its surface, instruments listening beneath the ground, and probes traveling far beyond the planets.

They were built from metal, wire, careful questions, and a little courage. We sent them to look, to listen, and to go farther than we could. Here, each machine gets to tell the story of what it found — and what it left behind.

“A mission can end. A discovery keeps moving.”
